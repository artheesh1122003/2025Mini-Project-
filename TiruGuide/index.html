About Tiruvannamalai

Tiruvannamalai is a world-renowned pilgrimage destination located in Tamil Nadu, India. It is the abode of Lord Shiva, worshipped here as Arunachaleswarar in the form of Fire (Agni). The magnificent temple at the foot of the Annamalai Hill is one of the largest in India.

Celebrated for the Girivalam path (circumambulation of the hill) and the Karthigai Deepam festival, this sacred city attracts millions of devotees seeking spiritual awakening and peace.
