Meet Our CEO

Leading the way in spiritual tourism innovation.

CEO

Ameer

Student at SKP Arts and Science College

Ameer is a visionary young leader currently pursuing his studies at SKP Arts and Science College. Driven by a deep spiritual connection to Tiruvannamalai and a passion for technology, he founded TiruGuide to bridge the gap between tradition and modern convenience.

Observing the challenges faced by millions of pilgrims who visit the holy city annually, Ameer conceptualized TiruGuide as a one-stop digital companion. From simplifying Girivalam with curated wellness kits to ensuring connectivity through power bank rentals, his innovations are centered around the devotee's experience.

"My mission is simple: to serve every seeker who steps onto this sacred land. When you walk the path of Girivalam, I want you to focus solely on the divine, knowing that TiruGuide has taken care of the rest."
